## 🌟 About the Podcast

Every month, we bring together the AI community in Lisbon for an engaging live podcast recording featuring a special guest. Our events combine:

- **In-depth conversations** with AI experts and thought leaders
- **Live Q&A sessions** with the audience
- **Networking opportunities** with fellow AI enthusiasts
- **Real-world insights** into AI applications and trends

Whether you're an AI researcher, developer, business leader, or simply curious about artificial intelligence, our events offer valuable insights and meaningful connections.
